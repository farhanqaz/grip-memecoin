Supply follows grip.

Every memecoin's supply is set by its deployer. Here it is set by the one thing a community controls: how much of the live float it keeps locked. Lock 60% and the token mints, paid to the lockers. Fall under 35% and it burns, out of a reserve the contract owns — never out of holders' pockets. No owner mint, no pause, no setters.

What happens at an epoch boundary

01
Locks that reached their deadline release first

Grip has to be maintained, not set once. An untouched lock stops counting before the rule is evaluated, so a passive holder cannot freeze the metric above target forever.

02
Grip ≥ 60% → mint 1% of supply

Paid pro-rata to whoever is locked, and paid out unlocked: the locked total always equals the sum of positions, so rewards never inflate the principal that earns them.

03
Grip ≤ 35% → burn 2% of supply

Drawn only from the contract-owned reserve. When the reserve is spent, the epoch records hold instead — the burn budget is finite by construction.

04
Anyone can call it

tick() is permissionless and unblockable. No admin key, no pause, no upgrade path: after new() the deployer has no power over the numbers.
